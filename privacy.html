XER CrossCheck: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains, in plain English, what XER CrossCheck ("the app", "we") does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app runs on your own device, it does not connect to the internet to do its work, it does not save or send your programmes anywhere, and we do not collect anything about you.

The summary

  • XER CrossCheck works offline. The two programme files you compare are read on your own device and are never uploaded.
  • The app never changes your files. It only reads them.
  • AMELIE·AI has no servers. We do not collect, receive, see or store your programmes or any personal information, and we have nothing to sell or share.
  • The app has no AI features, no advertising, no tracking and no account or sign-in.

1. Your programme files

XER CrossCheck compares two revisions of the same programme, read from files you choose: Primavera P6 files (.xer or P6 XML) and Microsoft Project XML files. The files are opened and compared on your own device, in the app's memory. They are not uploaded, because we have no servers to upload them to, and the app does not change, move or delete them.

The app does not keep a copy of your files or of the comparison. When you close it, what was on screen is gone, unless you saved a report yourself (see section 2).

2. Reports you save

When you choose to, the app saves the comparison as an Excel file, a small verification file (JSON, holding the two files' SHA-256 fingerprints) or a printout. These are made on your device and saved where you choose. They are yours: who you share them with is up to you, and we never see them.

3. We do not collect personal information

XER CrossCheck contains no analytics, tracking or telemetry of your usage. We do not collect your name, email, location, usage statistics or any other personal information. There is no account system and no database of users. Because we hold no user data, there is nothing for us to sell, share or pass to anyone, and we never do.

4. Offline storage of the app itself

So that it opens without the internet, the app keeps its own files (the app page, its icons and its settings file) on your device. This holds no information about you or your programmes.

5. Getting the app

If you get the app from the Microsoft Store, anything to do with the Store, your Microsoft account and your order is handled by Microsoft under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your account or payment details.

6. Children

XER CrossCheck is a professional tool for construction planning and programme review, intended for adult and business use. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
